2.3 : 
- Angular Speed for NavMeshAgent is the rotation speed
- Stopping distance is the distance from the target position that the agent will stop moving
- Quality determines how responsive the detection between NavMeshAgeent. "None" to both 
agent will not detect each other and they can walk through each other.
- NavMeshAgent with lower prioriy can push agent with same or higher priority, and they wont
consider calculating avoidance. Vice versa, agent with higher priority cant push agent with lower priority.
